    Gestures in VR

    Only a hand you can see counts. Hands on your lap do nothing. Raise a hand to chin height to use it.

    Open the menuDouble pinch: two quick pinches with one hand (thumb tip on index tip).
    Press a buttonPoint at it: a ray comes out of your hand. Then pinch.
    Close the menuPress X, pinch anywhere else, or look away.
    Pause or playPrayer hands: press your palms together and hold them a moment.
    Skip forward 30 sFlick your right hand toward the middle, hand open. Flick again to skip more.
    Go back 30 sFlick your left hand toward the middle, hand open.
    Zoom (VR180)Pinch with both hands. Move them apart or together.
    Turn the picture (VR180)Pinch with one hand and move it.
    Move the screen (flat)Pinch with one hand and move it.
    Resize the screen (flat)Pinch with both hands. Move them apart or together.

    The menu

    • Top row: Home (back to the list), -10 s, Play or Pause, +10 s, X.
    • Seek bar: point at a place and pinch, or pinch and drag.
    • Speed: − and + change it by 0.05. Or press 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 1.75× or 2×. The next video plays at the same speed.
    • Subtitles and Audio: they show when the video has them.
    • View: projection, 3D layout, Reset view, and Passthrough (in room view).

    Subtitles and audio tracks

    • The laptop finds subtitle files next to the video (Film.en.srt), in a Subs folder, and the text subtitles inside the video. Picture subtitles (DVD and Blu-ray) cannot show.
    • Subtitles too early or too late? On the Subtitles page of the menu, press Earlier or Later.
    • For another audio track, the laptop sends that track as a second sound stream. Your choice of language stays for the next video.
    • A video from this headset can get its subtitle file: pick both together in Choose a video, or open the video, then Add a file. The page cannot open a file you did not pick, so it cannot find the subtitles by itself.

    Problems

    • Hands do nothing in VR: use the Meta Quest Browser. Tap the icon at the left of the address bar, set Hand tracking to Allow, and reload this page.
    • The picture stays grey behind the Meta menu: wait one minute. Wandless then leaves VR by itself. Press Watch in VR: the video goes on where it was.
    • A video does not play: MP4 or MKV with H.264, H.265 or AV1 video and AAC, AC3 or EAC3 sound works best. DTS sound does not play.
    • The picture looks wrong: change the projection or the 3D layout, on the video's page or on the View page of the menu.
    • A passthrough video shows its coloured background, or looks see-through in your room: on the video's page, look at Your room. A filled background colour is found and removed by itself; for a black one, choose Remove a black background.
